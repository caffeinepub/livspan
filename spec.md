# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Remove the three evaluation metrics (Today, This Week, Current Streak) from the fasting panel in the LivSpan dashboard.

**Planned changes:**
- Remove the "Heute" (Today) metric display from the IntermittentFastingPanel evaluation section
- Remove the "Diese Woche" (This Week) metric display from the IntermittentFastingPanel evaluation section
- Remove the "Aktuelle Serie" (Current Streak) metric display from the IntermittentFastingPanel evaluation section

**User-visible outcome:** The fasting panel no longer shows the Today, This Week, and Current Streak metrics. The countdown timer, progress bar, fasting phase indicator, and schedule settings remain visible and functional with no empty gaps left behind.
